Unsupervised Learning:

K-Means

Learn how the k-means algorithm works

K-means is an unsupervised learning algorithm used to group data into clusters based on similarity. It doesn’t need labeled data — it just looks for patterns and similarities in the data points and divides the data points into groups that are similar to each other.

This is useful when you want to discover natural groupings in your data. For example, a company could use K-means to segment its customers into different groups based on their purchasing behavior, then send different targeted marketing emails to each group based on their shopping habits.

K-means is a simple but powerful algorithm for making meaningful groups in large, unlabeled datasets.
